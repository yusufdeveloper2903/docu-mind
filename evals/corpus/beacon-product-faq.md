# Beacon Product FAQ

Beacon is the (fictional) home air-quality monitor made by Lumen Labs. This FAQ is sample data for the DocuMind demo.

## Sensors and Accuracy

### What does Beacon measure?

Beacon measures fine particulate matter (PM2.5), carbon dioxide (CO2), volatile organic compounds (VOC), temperature and relative humidity. It does not detect carbon monoxide, so it is not a replacement for a carbon monoxide alarm.

### How accurate is the CO2 sensor?

Beacon uses a non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) CO2 sensor that is accurate to within ±50 ppm plus 3% of the reading, across a range of 400 to 5,000 ppm.

### How often does Beacon take a reading?

Beacon samples the air every 10 seconds and uploads a one-minute average to the cloud.

## Battery and Power

### How long does the battery last?

On a full charge the battery lasts up to 14 days with the display set to auto-dim. Charging from empty to full takes about 3 hours over USB-C.

### Can Beacon run without the battery?

Yes. Beacon can stay plugged into USB-C permanently; the battery then acts as a backup during power cuts.

## Connectivity

### Which Wi-Fi networks does Beacon support?

Beacon connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only (802.11 b/g/n). 5 GHz networks are not supported. Enterprise networks that require a username and password login (WPA2-Enterprise) are not supported either.

### Does Beacon work offline?

Yes. Without Wi-Fi Beacon keeps showing live readings on its display and stores up to 7 days of history locally, which is uploaded once the connection returns.

## Warranty and Returns

### What warranty does Beacon have?

Beacon comes with a 2-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. The battery is covered for 1 year. Damage from water or drops is not covered.

### Can I return Beacon?

You can return Beacon within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided it is undamaged and in its original packaging. Return shipping is free within the EU.

## Pricing

### How much does Beacon cost?

Beacon costs 149 EUR. The optional Beacon+ subscription costs 4 EUR per month and adds unlimited history, weekly air-quality reports and alerts by SMS.
