# Lumen Labs Employee Handbook

Lumen Labs is a fictional 60-person hardware startup that builds the Beacon home air-quality monitor. This handbook is sample data for the DocuMind demo.

## Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you are free to arrange your day however suits you, as long as your team knows when you are reachable.

Meetings should not be scheduled on Wednesdays. Focus Wednesday exists so that everyone gets one uninterrupted day for deep work each week.

## Remote Work

Every employee may work remotely. Employees who work from home receive a one-time home office stipend of 1,200 EUR, paid with the first salary, to spend on a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment.

If you want to work from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year, you need written approval from People Operations first, because longer stays can create tax obligations.

## Leave

### Vacation

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

### Sick Leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. A doctor's note is only required from the fourth consecutive day of sick leave.

### Parental Leave

Lumen Labs offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to every new parent, regardless of gender, including adoptive parents. Parental leave can be split into at most two blocks within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel counts against the same budget.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims in the finance tool within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the receipt attached. Claims under 50 EUR are approved automatically; larger claims need your manager's approval. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Equipment

New employees choose either a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
