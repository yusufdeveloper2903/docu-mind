Lumen Labs Information Security Policy (sample data for the DocuMind demo)

Passwords and accounts. Every account must use a password of at least 14 characters, generated and stored in the company password manager, 1Password. Passwords must never be reused between services. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source code hosting, the cloud console and the HR portal; hardware security keys are required for anyone with production access.

Devices. Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Personal devices may be used for email and chat only if they are enrolled in the company's mobile device management.

Data classification. Company data falls into three classes: public, internal and confidential. Customer air-quality readings and personal data are confidential. Confidential data may only be stored in approved systems and must never be pasted into unapproved third-party tools, including AI assistants that are not on the approved vendor list.

Access reviews. Managers review the access rights of their team every quarter. Access to production systems is removed within 24 hours when an employee changes role or leaves the company.

Incident reporting. Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails that were clicked, lost devices and accidental data sharing, must be reported in the #security-incidents Slack channel or to security@lumenlabs.example within 1 hour of discovery. Reporting quickly is always the right call; nobody is blamed for reporting an honest mistake.

Vendors. Any new vendor that will process confidential data needs a security review before a contract is signed. The review normally takes 10 business days.
